Common questions

What can you do at my home?

Anything done with a laptop: ECU remapping and tuning, gearbox software, diagnostics, programming, coding and DPF/EGR/AdBlue diagnostics. Mechanical repairs like servicing, brakes, clutches and timing belts are done at our garage.

Do you check the car before remapping?

Yes. We run diagnostics before and after every remap to make sure the car is healthy.

Do I need to tell my insurer about a remap?

It's a good idea to tell your insurer. We'll tell you exactly what was changed so you can give them the details.

Do you work on vans and 4x4s?

Yes: cars, vans and 4x4s, all makes and models.

Where is the garage?

We're in Dublin. Tap "Directions to garage" and Google Maps will take you straight there.

How do I pay?

Cash or Revolut, once the job is finished.
